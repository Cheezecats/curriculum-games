# Curriculum coverage and sources

This document records selected-topic connections. It is not a complete IB course map. The game’s original tasks, explanations, and questions are independent educational material.

## Physics: first assessment 2025

The public [IB Physics guide](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/subject-guides/physics-guide.pdf) identifies A.1 kinematics, A.2 forces and momentum, A.3 work/energy/power, and A.4 rigid body mechanics. A.4 has additional-HL content and no SL content. Topic references below use the guide’s own numbering; the task summaries are original.

| Mission                  | What the player measures                                              | Curriculum connection                             |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Glide through the valley | Time, displacement, speed and altitude during a real glide            | A.1, SL + HL                                      |
| A measured push          | Acceleration from 10 N and measured aircraft mass                     | A.2, SL + HL                                      |
| Precious cargo           | Payload, weight, tilt, and a delivery-gate crossing                   | A.2, SL + HL; aerodynamic stability is enrichment |
| Make every joule count   | Distance, time and actual electrical energy consumed                  | A.3, SL + HL; motor/airfoil design is enrichment  |
| Find the balance         | Angular acceleration from the same force through or away from COM     | A.4, HL                                           |
| Mass, spread out         | Equal-mass layout inertia and angular acceleration under equal torque | A.4, HL                                           |

Free flight is more complicated than the idealized problems used to verify the mathematical model. The controlled bench removes gravity and aerodynamic forces so the effect of a known force or torque can be isolated. Practical aircraft construction is an application and enrichment context.

## Computer Science: first assessment 2027

The official [IB curriculum update](https://ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/computer-science-updates/) confirms A.4 machine learning in the course first assessed in 2027. The public overview does not establish every detailed sub-objective or its SL/HL level. The UI deliberately marks these mappings **Scope review pending** and does not publish invented detailed objective IDs.

| Mission                   | Learning mechanism                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A line in the sand        | Train a linear classifier and inspect weights, bias and decision boundary |
| Around the corner         | Compare a hidden-layer network on a nonlinear sensor task                 |
| The label repair shop     | Audit corrupted training labels and evaluate on independent examples      |
| Little windows, big clues | Train a CNN and inspect actual learned convolution activations            |
| The night shift           | Augment training families and evaluate separately shifted test families   |
| Small robot, big job      | Compare measured accuracy with parameter-count constraints                |

Further verification of detailed objective/level mapping is required before replacing the pending labels. A mission’s working machine-learning mechanism is independently tested and does not imply an assessment specification.

Technical background: [TensorFlow.js models and layers](https://www.tensorflow.org/js/guide/models_and_layers), [training in a worker](https://www.tensorflow.org/js/tutorials/training/web_worker), and [platform/backends](https://www.tensorflow.org/js/guide/platform_environment).

## What the notebook means

- **Explored:** a finished attempt produced a recorded observation.
- **Mission complete:** that attempt satisfied the game’s measured objective.
- **Concept checked:** the learner answered an optional transfer question correctly.

These statuses are deliberately separate. They describe activity and evidence in the application, not validated mastery, exam readiness, or a measured improvement in learning.
